01 / Mission

We don’t teach the past.
We build the future.

We are building the bridge between traditional education and the future of artificial intelligence.

01

Learn what matters now

Curriculum shaped around today’s tools, standards and workplace realities — reviewed every year, because tax legislation, CRA procedures and AI tooling all change quickly.

02

Practice before it counts

Realistic scenarios turn knowledge into repeatable professional skill. Coursework uses simulated client files, so you practise on real problems without real exposure.

03

Lead the change

Use AI responsibly — not as a shortcut, but as a professional advantage. Every AI course covers privacy, limitations, and where human review is mandatory.

03 / One organization, two divisions

From education
to innovation.

ARSC operates an education division and a professional services division, run separately, under one corporate structure.

Division One

ARSC Professional Development College

Practical education for modern business. Online professional development across four schools.

  • Artificial intelligence and AI applications
  • Canadian taxation and accounting
  • Bookkeeping and payroll administration
  • Business management and startup
  • Human resources and administration

ARSC Accounting, Tax & Business Advisory

Professional support for individuals and growing businesses, delivered separately from the college.

ARSC provides non-degree professional development, continuing education and skills-upgrading courses. Certificates issued by ARSC confirm successful completion of private professional development training and do not represent an academic degree, government licence, regulated professional designation or provincially recognized career-training diploma.

ARSC courses are not a substitute for regulated accounting, CPA, legal or human resources professional designations. Legal advice, immigration advice and regulated accounting opinions are referred to appropriately licensed professionals.
